Care stays with the people who know them

Quiet Grid keeps the day clear: what’s coming up, who said yes, and what only the family should see.

  • Today and Plan

    Appointments and asks in one place — without a crowded dashboard.

  • Ask for help

    Propose a lift or a form; family can take an open request when nobody’s on it yet.

  • Invite by email

    Choose exactly what each person can see — editors, viewers, or helpers on shared appointments.

  • Family updates

    Short notes for the people organising care — not a public social feed.

  • Their own quieter screen

    Optional large-type home for the person you’re looking after, when you set it up.

Coordination stays free. Plans, requests, updates, people and their own screen don’t need OurNext Plus. Plus is optional for extra storage, a handover pack and more spaces you manage.
